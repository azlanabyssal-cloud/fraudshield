🚔 This is a Digital Arrest Scam — You Have Not Done Anything Wrong

  1. Hang up immediately — no matter what they threaten. "Digital Arrest" does not exist in Indian law. PM Modi confirmed this on Mann Ki Baat. No C B I, police, or court ever arrests anyone via video call.
  2. Call 1930 right now (free, 24×7). If money was transferred, report it immediately: the faster you report, the better the chance the receiving account can be frozen through I4C's CFCFRMS system.
  3. Call your bank's 24×7 helpline and say: "I was scammed — please freeze my last outgoing transaction immediately."
  4. File at cybercrime.gov.in as soon as you can. Upload all screenshots, call recordings, and fake warrant images they showed you.
  5. Tell your family — fraudsters often call other family members to add pressure. Everyone must know this is a scam right now.

In July 2025 a court in Nadia, West Bengal, gave nine members of a digital arrest network life sentences; the prosecutor called it India's first such conviction. The law is on your side.

💸 OTP / UPI Fraud — Act Right Now

  1. Call 1930 right now. CFCFRMS can freeze stolen funds in real-time — but only before the money moves out of the first account. Every minute matters.
  2. Call your bank's 24×7 helpline. Say: "Freeze my account — unauthorised transaction happened." Get a written complaint reference number.
  3. Change your UPI PIN, net banking password, and SIM PIN from a different device immediately. The fraudster may still have access.
  4. File at cybercrime.gov.in with all UTR numbers, transaction IDs, and the phone number that called you. Upload screenshots of all messages.
  5. File a police FIR at your nearest station under IT Act Sec 66C. Ask the bank whether RBI's limited-liability rules apply to you: they protect customers who report quickly when the fraud was not their fault.

RBI circular of 6 July 2017: if the fraud came from a breach that was not your fault and you report within 3 working days, your liability is zero; if you shared your OTP or PIN, you bear the loss until you report, and any loss after you report is the bank's.

🎣 Phishing Link Clicked — Secure Everything Now

  1. If you entered a bank login or OTP on that page — disconnect from the internet immediately and change all passwords from a clean device.
  2. Change passwords for your bank, email, UPI app, and any account linked to your phone number — do it now, not later.
  3. Enable two-factor authentication on all accounts. Use your registered mobile number as the second factor.
  4. Call your bank and inform them a phishing attempt occurred. Ask them to watch for any suspicious transactions and freeze your account if needed.
  5. Report the phishing URL to cybercrime.gov.in and CERT-In (cert-in.org.in). This protects others from the same link.

If you only clicked the link but did not enter any information — you are most likely safe. But run a full virus scan with an updated antivirus app as a precaution.

💰 Investment App Fraud — Your Money May Still Be Recoverable

  1. Stop depositing money immediately — no matter what "guaranteed profit" is shown. The dashboard is fake software designed to keep you investing until they disappear.
  2. Call 1930 and report all transaction IDs of the deposits you made. Provide app names and contact details of the "agents".
  3. Collect all evidence before the app disappears: screenshots of your "portfolio", all chat history, names of agents who contacted you, payment receipts.
  4. File at cybercrime.gov.in with full transaction history. Investment fraud is now "organised crime" under BNS 2023 Sec 318 — serious sentences apply.
  5. File a police FIR with all evidence. SEBI (sebi.gov.in) also has a complaint portal for investment fraud.

In 2025, investment fraud was 76% of the money reported lost in India, about ₹17,096 Cr. You are not alone: report it, and if any of the money was frozen you can apply to get it back.

🚫 Sextortion — Do Not Pay. Payment Guarantees They Return.

  1. Do not pay a single rupee. This is the most critical step. Payment tells them you will pay again — they always come back for more, often double or triple the amount.
  2. Block and report the person on every platform — WhatsApp, Instagram, Telegram. Screenshot all threats first before blocking.
  3. Call 1930 and tell them this is a sensitive case. You are the victim, and reporting early is the right step.
  4. File at cybercrime.gov.in — the portal has a dedicated sextortion section. All your personal details are sealed. Screenshots of threats are your evidence.
  5. If you are a woman — call 181 (free). The National Commission for Women provides immediate legal support and can connect you with trained cyber counsellors.

Threatening to share someone's private images to get money is a crime, and you are the victim. Do not pay, keep the messages as evidence, and report it.

🏦 Fake Loan App Harassment — You Have Strong Legal Protection

  1. Do not pay any "processing fee", "insurance charge", or "security deposit" — these are extraction tactics. No legitimate lender charges fees before disbursing a loan.
  2. Uninstall the app immediately and revoke all permissions it has to your contacts, camera, and gallery through your phone Settings → Apps.
  3. File at cybercrime.gov.in with the app name, screenshots of all threats and messages, and any payment receipts. This triggers investigation.
  4. Call 1930 and report the app. The RBI has banned hundreds of illegal digital lending apps — this one will be added to the list.
  5. If they send messages to your contacts — file an FIR under IT Act Sec 67 (obscene publication). This is a criminal offence with jail time.

RBI Digital Lending Guidelines (2022) prohibit fee collection before loan disbursement. If this happened — you were defrauded. File the complaint. The law covers you completely.

What is Digital Fraud Under Indian Law?

Any unlawful act where a mobile, computer, or network steals your money or identity. Prosecuted under IT Act 2000 (Sec 66C, 66D) and BNS 2023 (Sec 318, 319) since July 1, 2024. Offenders face up to 3–7 years imprisonment.

Cosmos Bank hack — Pune

₹94 crore taken on 11 and 13 August 2018

Hackers attacked the bank's card-payment system with malware and its SWIFT link. About ₹78 crore went out in more than 12,000 ATM withdrawals outside India, ₹13.92 crore was sent to a firm in Hong Kong, and about ₹2.5 crore was taken in transactions inside India. Kerala AI deepfake call — Kozhikode

₹40,000 lost to a fake video call

A retired executive in Kozhikode got a WhatsApp video call showing what looked like a friend, asking urgently for money for a sister's surgery. He sent ₹40,000, and grew suspicious only when the caller asked for ₹30,000 more. Kerala Police described it as the first case of its kind in Kerala to use AI-made video.

Nadia digital arrest — West Bengal

About ₹1 crore lost · 9 sentenced to life

A retired scientist was told by a caller posing as Mumbai Police that he was under "digital arrest" and moved about ₹1 crore across several accounts. Know Before It Happens

6 Frauds Draining Indian Savings Right Now

QR codes only take money out
📱

UPI Fraud

QR codes only DEBIT your account. Scanning one to "receive" money is physically impossible by design. If anyone says otherwise — hang up.

Never share an OTP
🔒

OTP Scam

No bank, RBI, NPCI, or government body ever asks for your OTP. Sharing it gives complete account control. Hang up, call your bank directly.

Check the web address
🎣

Phishing

One wrong letter in a URL steals your login. "sbi-kyc-update.net" vs "sbi.co.in". Always type the address yourself. Never click SMS or WhatsApp links.

Uninstall it, then tell someone
💰

Fake Loan Apps

Any loan app requesting contacts or media access is a blackmail trap. It harvests contacts and morphs photos to extort you. Uninstall immediately.

🚫

Sextortion

Do not pay a single rupee. Payment guarantees they return. Call 1930 immediately and say it is a sensitive case.

💼

Investment Fraud

Real investments carry risk, so anyone promising guaranteed returns is a red flag. A big profit shown on a dashboard can be fabricated by the app itself.

Know Your Rights · Indian Law

The Law Protects You.
Use It.

Digital fraud is a serious crime. Offenders face imprisonment and fines. Know your rights before you need them.

IT Act 2000 · Sec 66C

Identity Theft

Fraudulently using another person's electronic signature, password or other unique identification feature.

Up to 3 years · fine up to ₹1 lakh

IT Act 2000 · Sec 66D

Cheating by Personation

Anyone cheating you by pretending to be a bank officer, CBI agent, police, or any person via computer or mobile device.

Up to 3 years · fine up to ₹1 lakh

BNS 2023 · Sec 318 & 319

Digital Fraud & Impersonation

Replaced IPC Sections 415–420 from 1 July 2024. BNS recognises cybercrime as "organized crime" — enabling prosecution of entire fraud syndicates.

Cheating (318(4)): up to 7 years · Organised crime (111): 5 years to life
