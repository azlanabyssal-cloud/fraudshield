PREVENTION GUIDE · UPDATED 2026

How To Protect Yourself

Real scenarios from cases reported in India. Plain words, in the order you need them.

UPI Fraud

A QR code can only take money out of your account

HOW IT HAPPENS

A scammer poses as a buyer, helpline agent, or sender and shares a UPI collect request or QR code saying "scan this to receive ₹X." Scanning approves a payment FROM your account — you send money, not receive it.

HOW TO STAY SAFE

Receiving money never requires scanning a QR code or entering your UPI PIN. If anyone asks you to scan to receive payment — disconnect immediately and call 1930.

QR codes only debit your account. To receive money, just share your UPI ID or mobile number.

OTP Scam

No bank or agency ever needs your OTP

HOW IT HAPPENS

A caller claims to be from your bank, TRAI, CBI, or police and creates urgency: "your SIM will be blocked in 2 hours unless you verify with your OTP." The OTP is used to authorize a transaction from your account.

HOW TO STAY SAFE

No bank, regulator, or government body ever asks for your OTP. It is a One-Time Password meant only for you. The moment any caller asks for it, hang up immediately.

OTP is like your ATM PIN. No one — not your bank, not RBI, not CBI — will ever ask for it.

Phishing

One wrong letter in a web address is the giveaway

HOW IT HAPPENS

You receive an SMS: "SBI KYC incomplete. Update here: sbi-kyc-update.net — or your account will be blocked." The page looks identical to your bank. Every detail you enter goes directly to criminals.

HOW TO STAY SAFE

SBI's real domain is sbi.co.in. Government sites end in .gov.in. IRCTC is irctc.co.in. Bookmark these. Never click a link in any SMS or WhatsApp message — always type the URL yourself.

Check the URL bar before entering any password. One wrong character = a fake site.

Fake Loan Apps

Aimed at people in financial distress

HOW IT HAPPENS

App offers instant ₹5,000–₹50,000 loan with no documents. After install, it reads your entire contact list and photo gallery. When repayment is missed, they morph your photos and send them to every person in your contacts.

HOW TO STAY SAFE

Only borrow from institutions on RBI's official approved lenders list at rbi.org.in. If any app demands access to your contacts or photos to process a loan, uninstall it immediately.

RBI-approved lenders never access your contacts. Contact permission request for a loan = danger.

Sextortion

19% of cases · 4% of the money lost · 2025

HOW IT HAPPENS

A stranger with an attractive profile photo video calls you. The call is recorded. Hours later, they threaten to send the recording to your family and employer unless you transfer money immediately.

HOW TO STAY SAFE

Never accept video calls from unknown numbers. Do not pay even one rupee — payment proves you can pay and demands will increase. Report to 1930 immediately. Cases are handled with full confidentiality and no judgment.

Do not pay. Do not respond. Call 1930. The team handles these cases discreetly.

Job & Investment Fraud

76% of the money lost in 2025 — more than any other type

HOW IT HAPPENS

WhatsApp message: work-from-home ₹500 per task. Small early payouts build trust. Then they ask you to deposit ₹5,000–₹50,000 to "unlock bigger earnings." The money disappears and the account goes silent.

HOW TO STAY SAFE

Genuine employers do not charge you to give you a job. Any investment promising guaranteed returns is a major red flag for a Ponzi scheme, because real investments carry risk.

Real jobs pay you first. Guaranteed high returns = guaranteed scam.
IF YOU ARE ALREADY A VICTIM

Here Is Exactly What To Do
— Step by Step

The sooner you report, the better the chance the money can be frozen. Every minute matters.

The National Cyber Crime Helpline 1930 is operational 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. It is free. Tell them: the exact amount transferred, the UPI ID or bank account number, the time and date, and the app or bank you used. They can immediately issue a hold request to the receiving bank before the funds are moved to a secondary account.

Call 1930 Now

Go to cybercrime.gov.in and click "Report Financial Fraud." You will need: your mobile number, the amount lost, the bank or UPI app name, and screenshots of any messages or transactions. The 24-hour window is critical — this is when banks can still intercept funds in transit. After 24 hours, reversal becomes extremely difficult.

File at cybercrime.gov.in →

Call the 24-hour helpline on the back of your debit card. Say: "I am a fraud victim and I want to report an unauthorised transaction." Under RBI circular RBI/2017-18/15 (6 July 2017), if the fraud came from a breach that was not your fault and you report within 3 working days, your liability is zero; if you shared your OTP or PIN, you bear the loss until you report, and any loss after you report is the bank's. Ask for a written complaint number before ending the call.

Screenshot every SMS, WhatsApp message, call log, app notification, and bank transaction related to the fraud. Note the exact time, date, and everything the scammer said. Save the UPI transaction ID or bank reference number. Write it all down on paper too — do not rely only on your phone. Do not delete anything — this is your legal evidence.

Visit your nearest police station and ask specifically for the Cybercrime Cell. Bring: your Aadhaar card, the complaint reference number from cybercrime.gov.in, your bank statement showing the fraud transaction, and all screenshots. An FIR creates the official legal record that supports both your bank reversal claim and any future prosecution of the fraudsters.

Freezing stops the money moving; it does not return it. Since April 2026 the Money Restoration Module on the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in) lets you apply to get frozen money back. You need the 14-digit complaint number from your report, the mobile number you reported with (it sends an OTP), your PAN, and your bank account number and IFSC. Reports say a police report is enough when the frozen amount in a single account is up to ₹50,000, and that an FIR is compulsory above that. Only money that was frozen before it was withdrawn can be returned, and recovery is not guaranteed. Keep the tracking number you are given.

You are not alone. You did nothing wrong.

Professional fraud networks operate with rehearsed scripts and psychological playbooks. The victims in reported cases include a retired scientist and a retired executive: careful, educated people. Getting help immediately is the correct and courageous response.
