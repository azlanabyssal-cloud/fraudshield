Fraud Type Breakdown 2023

Source: Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C) — MHA 2023

  • UPI / Payment Fraud — 67%
    Most common. Fake collect requests, QR codes, malicious apps.
  • OTP & SIM Swap — 12%
    Caller tricks victim into sharing one-time password.
  • Phishing & Fake Sites — 9%
    Fake bank and government websites steal login credentials.
  • Fake Loan Apps — 7%
    Apps steal contacts and photos then blackmail victims.
  • Other — 5%
    Sextortion, job fraud, investment scams, digital arrest.
📌 UPI fraud alone (67%) is more than all other fraud types combined. India processes 13.9 billion UPI transactions monthly — making this the largest payment fraud surface in the world.

FORENSIC TIMELINE · 2000–2026

How Digital Fraud Evolved in India

Four distinct eras, each shaped by a leap in technology — and each eventually met by a leap in the state's response.

2000–2010

Incubation & Anonymity

~70 registered cases in 2002

Fraud lived in public cyber cafés — Nigerian "419" advance-fee emails and rudimentary stalking. Low sophistication, high friction: India's banking sector wasn't yet digitised enough for fast money movement.

2011–2018

The Jamtara Vishing Boom

4,356 (2013) → 27,248 cases (2018)

Cheap smartphones decentralised fraud from urban hackers to rural syndicates. The "Jamtara model" — named after a Jharkhand district — turned voice phishing into an industry: fake bank executives harvesting OTPs and CVVs by phone.

2019–2023

Real-Time Payments Exploitation

44,735 (2019) → 86,420 cases (2023)

UPI's frictionless rails became the getaway car. Fake "receive money" QR codes, remote-access apps disguised as customer support, and predatory loan apps harvesting entire contact lists for blackmail — some cases ending in tragedy.

2024–2026

Industrialisation & Synthetic Deception

₹22,845 Cr lost in 2024 — a 206% jump

AI-driven deepfake voice and video, multi-day psychological "digital arrests," and automated mule-account networks that launder stolen funds into crypto within minutes. The most sophisticated era yet — met by the most sophisticated countermeasures yet.

THE STATE FIGHTS BACK

India's Real-Time Defence Systems

Technology this precise didn't exist five years ago. Here's what's actually intercepting fraud right now.

🧭

Pratibimb

21,857 arrests

I4C's GIS-based tracking module maps the physical location of active fraud numbers in real time, giving police direct visibility into "fraud factories."

🤖

MuleHunter.AI

₹9,518 Cr blocked

RBI's machine-learning model flags mule accounts by behavioural pattern — rapid fund-out immediately after credit — independent of human review.

📋

Suspect Registry

27.37 lakh accounts flagged

A centralised ledger shared across banks and I4C, built from 23.05 lakh suspect data points reported by financial institutions since late 2024.

📵

SIM & Device Blocking

12.94 lakh SIMs · 3.03 lakh IMEIs

The Department of Telecommunications has permanently blacklisted this many fraud-linked SIM cards and device identities, cutting off the physical tools of the trade.

💰 New in 2026: the RBI has proposed an industry-funded compensation framework offering automatic payouts of up to ₹25,000 for small-value digital fraud victims — removing the burden of a lengthy investigation for the most common cases.
