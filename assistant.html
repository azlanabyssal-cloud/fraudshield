Talk to the FraudShield Assistant

Paste a message or link, or share a screenshot or QR code. It names the scam it resembles, quotes the exact words that gave it away, and walks you through what to do. Your messages and screenshots are processed in your browser and are not sent to our servers. Voice is the one exception, and only if you choose it: your browser's speech service hears the audio (never FraudShield), unless your browser can do it on the device. This page loads its fonts, scripts and images from this site only.
